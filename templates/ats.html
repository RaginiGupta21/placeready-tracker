{# INFO BOX - explain ATS to the student #}
What is ATS? Applicant Tracking System - software companies use to auto-filter resumes before a person reads them. Resumes that lack the keywords in the job description can be filtered out. Your resume needs to contain the right keywords to pass. This tool tells you which keywords are missing and gives you ready-to-paste sentences to add them.
